# Seed Data & Mock Dataset Generator

The course specification explicitly requires at least 500 articles across diverse categories, statuses, multi-revision histories, and realistic view analytics for the project defense demonstration.

## Features
1. **500+ Articles**: Spanning Technology, News, Economy, Sports, Culture, Health, World.
2. **User Personas**: 4 distinct reporters and 1 editor account.
3. **Status Distribution**: Drafts, Pending Review, Published, and Rejected with editorial notes.
4. **Impact Analytics Seed**: Generates historical hourly view curves with editor update milestones.
